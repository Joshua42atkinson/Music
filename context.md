# Voix Vive: Project Context & Philosophy

## What is Voix Vive?
Voix Vive ("The Living Voice") is an advanced, highly esoteric guitar mentorship platform designed by Bertrand Laurence. It rejects the modern paradigm of fast, gamified, dopamine-driven e-learning. Instead, it operates on the philosophy of the **"Slow Web"**—forcing students into a contemplative, deliberate, and somatically aware state of learning.

It is conceptualized as a **"Bard's Grimoire"**—a living textbook and peer-to-peer mentorship hub where the student learns that *they* are an instrument playing an instrument.

## 1. Core Pedagogical Philosophy

### Somatic Awareness (Yin) vs. Technique (Yang)
The curriculum is strictly balanced between Yin (internal) and Yang (external) practices.
- **Yin:** Meditation, breathwork, fascia release, and kinesthetic awareness. The student must learn to release physical tension before touching the instrument.
- **Yang:** Fretboard mapping, the CAGED system, and acoustic execution.

### The PLING! Protocol
A core mechanical philosophy of the curriculum. The student must never play a note blindly.
1. **Hear it:** Audiate the pitch internally.
2. **Sing it:** Vocalize the pitch to physically feel the resonance.
3. **Play it:** Execute the pitch on the fretboard, matching the internal and vocalized expectation.

## 2. The Visual Identity: "The Unbound Grimoire"
The aesthetic of Voix Vive is unapologetically esoteric, diverse, and imaginative. It avoids sterile stock photography.

**The Prompt Matrix:**
When generating AI art for the curriculum, we use an "Unbound" conceptual strategy:
- We lean heavily into surreal, metaphorical imagery (e.g., Alchemists forging notes, monks meditating on giant floating guitar picks, starry skies viewed through guitar soundholes).
- **Vibe:** "High-quality, imaginative, and esoteric pedagogical illustration. Deeply atmospheric and metaphorical. A mix of surrealism and grounded reality."
- **Constraint:** We do not restrict to "analog photography" to ensure the 112-slide curriculum remains visually stimulating and diverse.

## 3. The Shift to Mentorship
Initially conceived as a static masterclass website, Voix Vive is evolving into a full 1-on-1 mentorship ecosystem. The architecture is shifting to support asynchronous video homework (the "Shearl" loop), direct messaging, and live Socratic dialogue, transforming Bertrand from a static lecturer into an active Master/Mentor.

## 4. Current State
- **Phase 1 (Complete):** The frontend "Living Textbook" slides for Chapters 1-12 are wired with esoteric AI art.
- **Phase 2 (Active):** Transitioning to a Desktop-as-a-Server (DaaS) architecture to support the new Mentorship Hub features without relying on expensive cloud backends.
