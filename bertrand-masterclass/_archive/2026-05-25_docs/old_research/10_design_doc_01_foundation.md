# VOIX VIVE — Design Document | Batch 01: Foundation
*The Student, The Way, The Three Protocols*

---

## The Axiom

> *"You are an instrument playing an instrument. If I am playing the guitar — who is playing me?"*
> — Bertrand Laurence

Everything in this platform flows from that single question. The software does not teach guitar. It teaches the **student to hear themselves** — their tension, their breath, their creative impulse — and then, from that awareness, to play.

This document is the single source of truth for the Voix Vive platform. It is written for builders. Every technical decision traces back to a pedagogical one. Every pedagogical decision traces back to the student.

---

## I. THE STUDENT — Who Learns Here

### The Primary Learner

Adults aged 30–65. Most have tried before and stopped. They did not fail because they lacked talent. They failed because the system they entered was designed for children: rote drills, punitive grading, performance anxiety baked in from lesson one.

These students arrive carrying:
- **Fear of sounding bad** — especially in front of others
- **Impatience with their own hands** — they hear what they want, they can't execute it, and they interpret the gap as a personal failing
- **Fragmented time** — they practice in stolen moments: 10 minutes before dinner, 20 minutes on a weekend morning. Progress must survive those gaps
- **Inner critic dominance** — the analytical mind interrupts the creative one constantly

### What Actually Works for Them

Bertrand's method has a near-perfect retention rate precisely because it reverses the standard approach:

| Conventional Approach | Bertrand's Approach |
|---|---|
| Start with technique | Start with somatic safety |
| Judge performance by speed | Judge by presence and breath |
| Theory as prerequisite | Theory as discovery |
| Practice alone, perform later | Internalize before you externalize |
| Scales as drills | Scales as spatial stories |

The platform must embody the second column in every interaction. **No speed metrics. No streaks that punish. No leaderboards.** The only competition is between the student and their own previous moment of tension.

### The Secondary Learner: The Gift-Giver

A secondary user type purchases the platform as a gift. They do not play guitar. They need:
- A simple, printable or emailable confirmation of what was purchased
- A clear description of what their loved one will receive
- A gift certificate or code interface in the StudioPage

## II. THE WAY — Pedagogical Philosophy

### The Slow Web Mandate

Voix Vive is explicitly anti-dopamine. The interface never rushes the student. Transitions are slow. The ambient soundscape is continuous and non-urgent. The breath gate precedes every session. The platform simulates the psychological environment of a **physical practice nook** — a safe, quiet room where no one is watching and no one is judging.

This is the "Slow Web" philosophy in practice: depth over breadth, presence over pace.

### Yin and Yang: The Dual-Coding Engine

Every instructional moment in the curriculum has two simultaneous dimensions:

```
YIN (The Invisible Domain)         YANG (The Visible Domain)
─────────────────────────────      ─────────────────────────────
Abstract music theory              Fretboard geometry
Ear training & pitch memory        Finger placement & kinesthesis
Breath & nervous system regulation Physical ergonomics & relaxation
Emotional interpretation           Muscle memory & myelination
Creative impulse                   Technical execution
```

The platform's telemetry always measures both. A student who plays the right note with locked shoulders has only achieved Yang. The Yin signal (breath, tension, somatic ease) must be present for mastery to register.

### The Three Protocols: A Complete Learning Cycle

The three proprietary protocols are not separate tools. They are **a single, sequential learning cycle** that maps directly to the cognitive arc of skill acquisition:

```
PERCEIVE → EMBODY → EXPRESS
    ↓           ↓          ↓
 ©SHEARL    ©PLING!    ©FHEAL
```

**©SHEARL — See / Hear / Feel** *(Perception)*
> The bridge from theory to touch. Before the student places a finger, they see the pattern on the fretboard, hear the interval internally, and feel the physical shape in their hand's memory. This is always the entry point.

**©PLING! — Sing & Play** *(Embodiment)*
> The integration protocol. The student sings the pitch before they play it — hardwiring the vocal tract to the motor cortex. This is the most physiologically powerful intervention in the curriculum: it forces the student to *know* the sound before they execute it, bypassing visual dependency entirely.

**©FHEAL — Hear / Feel** *(Expression)*
> The liberation protocol. Once the student can SHEARL and PLING!, FHEAL trains them to bypass the prefrontal cortex's analytical interference and translate raw creative impulse directly into the instrument. This is where improvisation lives.

> **Platform note for builders:** Every interactive tool in the Digital Binder maps to one of these three protocols. The tool's telemetry should measure the student's state within that protocol's domain — not generic performance metrics.

### The Myelination Imperative

All gamification in Voix Vive is calibrated around one biological fact: **myelin sheaths form around neural pathways during slow, conscious, correct repetition — not fast, pressured drilling.** The Vertiscale Engine's scoring system (see Batch 03) is built entirely on this principle.
