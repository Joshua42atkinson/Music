# Bertrand Mentorship Integration Strategy

> **Goal:** To fill the "mentorship gap" in the Voix Vive Academy by maximizing Bertrand Laurence's presence through targeted video and voice instruction across the 12-fret curriculum.

## 1. The Bertrand Laurence Profile (Knowledge Synthesis)

### Background & Credentials
*   **Education:** Alumni of Berklee College of Music, MassArt, and Mirage Mime Theatre.
*   **Location:** Houlton, Maine (relocated from Cambridge/Boston, MA).
*   **Experience:** Master guitarist and vocalist, instructor at Passim School of Music (notably the "Hands-On Harmony" class), and a Thumbtack "Top Pro".
*   **Creative Work:** Solo performer, silent film scorer (e.g., Jean Renoir, Charlie Chaplin), and collaborator on unique projects like the "Aroostook Axe" custom guitar.
*   **Target Audience:** Adult learners (aged 30-65) who have often quit guitar previously due to performance anxiety, the "inner critic," or traditional methods designed for children.

### Core Pedagogy: "Somatic Mystic"
Bertrand’s teaching is built on a body-centered, ergonomic, and psychological approach. The foundational premise is: **"The student is not a broken instrument waiting to be repaired — they are the song that has not yet fully sounded."**

**The 3 Proprietary Protocols:**
1.  **©SHEARL (See/Hear/Feel):** The foundational input protocol. Visualizing the fretboard, hearing the interval internally before playing, and feeling the physical sensation.
2.  **©PLING! (Sing & Play):** The expression protocol. Vocalizing pitches alongside the guitar to bridge the inner ear and the instrument.
3.  **©FHEAL (Feel/Hear/Express/Act/Live):** The integration protocol. Moving from mechanical practice to emotional, lived performance.

**Key Concepts:**
*   **Notes > Chords > Songs:** Understanding how music works to release creativity, rather than blindly memorizing shapes.
*   **The Three Inners:** Inner Fretboard, Inner Ear, Inner Voice.
*   **Vertiscale:** Playing scales vertically up a single string to understand tension/resolution, rather than horizontally in "boxes".

## 2. Maximizing the Mentorship Presence

To make the Voix Vive platform feel like a living masterclass, Bertrand's physical presence (voice and video) must be strategically injected at critical pedagogical moments, specifically at the "Somatic Gates" of each fret.

### Video Phasing Strategy per Fret
Each of the 12 frets (chapters) requires a minimum of 3 distinct mentorship videos corresponding to the ADDIECRAPEYE / Day Dream phases:

1.  **BE (Imagine/Concept):** A philosophical, somatic introduction to the interval. Focuses on breathing, posture, and the emotional color of the sound.
2.  **DO (Listen/Practice):** A practical, over-the-shoulder demonstration of the specific exercise (e.g., Vertiscale, PitchRoom).
3.  **PLAY (Perform/Express):** A musical demonstration or call-to-action encouraging the student to record their own submission for mentor review.

## 3. The Video Curriculum Map (Production Checklist)

Below is the definitive list of videos Bertrand needs to record (or curate from his existing YouTube archives) to fully populate the `VideoLibrary.jsx`.

### FRET 1: The Root (Perfect Unison)
*   [ ] **1-BE: The Breath Before the Note.** Introduction to somatic posture. Breathing exercises before touching the guitar.
*   [ ] **1-DO: Hearing the Root.** How to internalize the E pitch. Plucking the open string with intention.
*   [ ] **1-PLAY: Your First Note.** A demonstration of playing a single note with absolute conviction and musicality.

### FRET 2: The Minor 2nd
*   [ ] **2-BE: The Sound of Tension.** The psychology of dissonance. Why the minor 2nd feels uncomfortable and why we shouldn't fear it.
*   [ ] **2-DO: Holding Tension.** A Vertiscale demonstration. Playing the open E, then the F, and physically holding the tension in the body.
*   [ ] **2-PLAY: The Jaws Motif.** Using the minor 2nd to create suspense. A short musical example.

### FRET 3: The Major 2nd
*   [ ] **3-BE: The Stepping Stone.** The concept of whole steps. The feeling of taking a full stride on the fretboard.
*   [ ] **3-DO: Vertiscale Introduction.** Bertrand explains why playing *up* the string (vertically) connects the ear better than playing across.
*   [ ] **3-PLAY: The Folk Foundation.** A simple melody using the Root, Major 2nd, and Minor 3rd.

### FRET 4: The Minor 3rd
*   [ ] **4-BE: The Color of Melancholy.** The emotional weight of the minor third. Introduction to the Blues/Folk feeling.
*   [ ] **4-DO: Left Hand Positioning.** Ergonomics: Thumb placement, wrist angle, and finger independence for the 3-fret stretch.
*   [ ] **4-PLAY: The Blues Bend.** A demonstration of slightly bending the minor 3rd for emotional expression.

### FRET 5: The Major 3rd
*   [ ] **5-BE: The Light Comes In.** The psychology of the major chord. Happiness, resolution, and stability.
*   [ ] **5-DO: The Double Stop.** Playing the Root and Major 3rd together. Listening for the harmonic resonance.

### FRET 6: The Perfect 4th

*   [ ] **6-PLAY: The Hymn.** A demonstration of playing fourths in a contemplative, sustained manner.

*   [ ] **7-BE: The Devil in Music.** The history and raw tension of the tritone. Embracing the darkest interval.
*   [ ] **7-DO: The Blues Scale.** Injecting the tritone into the minor pentatonic.
