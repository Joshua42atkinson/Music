## How This Map Works

Voix Vive is a **sandbox** — like a practice room with many doors. You choose which door to open. There are no tests you can fail, no timers pushing you forward, and no one watching you make mistakes. This map shows you what's behind each door so you feel comfortable exploring.

The app has **three main areas**, like three rooms in a house:

```
🏠 THE LANDING PAGE (Your Front Door)
    │
    ├── 🎵 THE SONG ──── Learn the "why" behind music (reading + history)
    │
    ├── 🎸 THE GUITAR ── Practice with your hands (games + tools)
    │
    └── 🧘 THE PLAYER ── Take care of yourself as a musician (breath + recording)
```

You can visit any room at any time. There's no required order.

## 🎸 Screen 3: The Guitar (The Vertiscale Engine)

**What it is:** This is the main practice game. It trains you to **see the guitar neck in your mind** — so your fingers can find notes without looking.

**What you see first:** A guitar neck menu with 12 root notes (C, C#, D, D#, E, F, F#, G, G#, A, A#, B). Each one opens a different scale pattern to practice.

### Getting Started

1. **Tap a root note** (start with C if you're new)
2. You'll see a "What is a Vertiscale?" panel — **tap it to learn what you're about to do**
3. Below that, you'll see **five game modes** organized into sections

---

### The Five Game Modes

#### ⚡ FLASH · Quick Recall
**Section:** The Inner Fretboard  
**What it trains:** Your visual memory of where notes live

**How it works:**
1. Gold dots appear on a mini guitar neck — these are the notes in the scale
2. You have a few seconds to memorize their positions
3. The dots disappear
4. **Tap where the dots were** from memory
5. The app shows you how you did:
   - 🟢 **Green** = You got it right
   - 🔴 **Red** = You tapped the wrong spot
   - 🟠 **Orange** = You missed a note entirely

**First time?** On your first round, a tutorial box appears explaining exactly what to do. Just read it — no rush.

**Scoring:** Your score is based on **accuracy, not speed.** The game cares about whether you placed the notes correctly, not how fast you did it. Breathe. Take your time.

> 🇫🇷 *ÉCLAIR — Des points dorés apparaissent puis disparaissent. Tapez de mémoire.*

---

#### 🫁 IMAGINE · Sustained Hold
**Section:** The Inner Fretboard  
**What it trains:** Your ability to hold a mental picture steady

**How it works:**
1. Gold dots appear on the neck and **stay visible**
2. Tap each dot to "lock in" your placement
3. **Hold your placement** — breathe steadily
4. A progress bar fills up as you hold. The longer you hold, the better
5. When the bar completes (or you tap "Release"), the app checks your accuracy

**The difference from Flash:** Flash tests how quickly you can recall. Imagine tests how **steadily** you can hold the picture in your mind. Both are important.

> 🇫🇷 *IMAGINER — Le motif reste visible. Placez vos doigts et respirez.*

---

#### 🎵 AUDIATE · Pling! Orbs
**Section:** The Inner Ear  
**What it trains:** Your ability to hear notes in your head and match them with your voice

**How it works:**
1. A colored orb (circle) with a note name appears at the top of the screen
2. The orb slowly drops down
3. When it reaches the "gate line," **sing or hum that note** into your phone/computer microphone
4. A pitch meter shows whether you're sharp (too high), flat (too low), or in tune
5. If your pitch matches: 🟢 the orb turns green
6. If not: 🟡 the orb turns amber (but you still continue — no punishment)
7. Then tap the orb to confirm

**First time?** You need to enable your microphone. A button at the top says "🎤 Enable Microphone (optional)" — tap it to turn on voice detection.

**Don't worry about your voice.** This isn't a singing test. The app just checks if the note you're imagining in your head matches what comes out. Humming works fine.

> 🇫🇷 *AUDIER — Un orbe descend. Chantez ou fredonnez la note avant de la toucher.*

---

#### 📝 REFLECT · Session Journal
**Section:** The Inner Voice  
**What it trains:** Your awareness of your own learning process

**What you see:**
1. A **score ring** showing your overall session accuracy (like a pie chart)
2. A **stats grid** showing: root note, number of rounds, best score, and streak status
3. A **round history bar chart** — each bar shows one round's score
4. A **coaching message** from Bertrand based on how you did
5. A **journal text area** where you can write about your experience

**What to write in the journal:** Anything. Some ideas:
- "I kept forgetting where the 4th string note was"
- "My breathing got shallow when the pattern got bigger"
- "The 3rd round felt easier — I could almost see it with my eyes closed"

Your journal entries are saved on your device. No one else can see them unless you share them.

**Buttons at the bottom:**
- "↻ Again" — Start another session with the same root note
- "Done" — Return to the game menu

> 🇫🇷 *RÉFLÉCHIR — Relisez vos résultats et notez vos observations dans le journal.*

---

#### 🏰 ADVENTURE · The Troubadour
**Section:** The Living Story  
**What it is:** An interactive story set in medieval France. At key moments, you must **match a musical pitch** to continue the story.

**How it works:**
1. You read a narrative scene (text + atmosphere)
2. At certain moments, a **pitch gate** appears — the story pauses
3. Sing or hum the target note into your microphone
4. If you match: the story continues
5. If you don't match within 25 seconds: the story still continues, but your score adjusts
6. At decision points, you choose between 2-3 story paths

**You cannot fail this mode.** The story always continues. The pitch gates are practice opportunities, not tests.

> 🇫🇷 *AVENTURE — Une histoire interactive dans la cour d'Aliénor d'Aquitaine, Poitiers, 1165.*

│  │   G ──┼──┼──●──┼──┼──┼──    │   │

**What the colors mean on the fretboard:**

| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 🟡 **Gold glow** | This is where a note is (during REVEAL phase) |
| ⚫ **Dark/empty** | No note here (or notes are hidden — it's DARK phase) |
| 🟢 **Green** | You tapped correctly! |
| 🔴 **Red pulse** | You tapped the wrong spot |
| 🟠 **Amber** | A note was here but you missed it |
| 💜 **Purple pulse** | Root note (the "home" note of the scale) |

---

### How the Game Gets Harder (Gradually)

The game starts **very simple** and only gets more complex as you improve:

| Stage | What Changes |
|-------|-------------|
| Stage 1 | Only the bottom 2 strings (E and A). Just a few notes. |
| Stage 2 | 3 strings. The pattern grows. |
| Stage 3 | 4 strings. The B string has an offset — this is normal! |
| Stage 4 | All 6 strings. The full vertical scale pattern. |

**Timing also adjusts:**
- Your first flash lasts **3.5 seconds** — plenty of time to look
- As you improve, flashes get shorter (down to 1 second)
- The game measures your **consistency** over 5 rounds, not your raw speed

> 🇫🇷 *Le jeu commence simplement et grandit avec vous. Pas de pression.*

## 🧘 Screen 4: The Player (Mentor Tools)

**What it is:** Your personal practice toolkit. These are standalone tools that help you practice specific skills.

**What you see:** A guitar neck menu with 12 frets, each containing a different practice tool.

### The 12 Practice Tools

| Fret | Tool Name | What It Does | How to Use It |
|------|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| 1 | **Breathing Gate** | Calms your nervous system before practice | Breathe in sync with the expanding/contracting circle. The gate "opens" when your breathing is steady. |
| 2 | **Practice Timer** | Sets a simple practice timer | Choose 5, 10, 15, or 20 minutes. A soft bell sounds when time is up. |
| 3 | **Pitch Room** | Tests your ability to hear pitch differences | Two notes play. Are they the same or different? Higher or lower? |
| 4 | **Metronome** | Keeps steady time for you | Tap to set a tempo, or type in a BPM. The click plays through your speakers. |
| 5 | **Interval Visualizer** | Shows the relationship between two notes | Pick two notes and see/hear the distance between them. |
| 6 | **Fretboard Explorer** | Interactive full guitar neck diagram | Tap any fret on any string to see/hear the note. Toggle scale overlays. |
| 7 | **PLING! Trainer** | Sing-then-play practice | The app shows a note. Sing it first, then find it on the fretboard. |
| 8 | **Microtonal Tracker** | Super-precise pitch meter | Sing or play — see your pitch accuracy down to individual cents (1/100th of a note). |
| 9 | **Vertiscale Engine** | The main practice game | (Same as "The Guitar" — opens the full game) |
| 10 | **Practice Recorder** | Record yourself practicing | Hit record, play, and listen back. Save recordings for later. |
| 11 | **Multi-Key Hub** | See all 12 keys at once | Compare scale shapes across different keys. |
| 12 | **Rhythm Engine** | Play along with backing tracks | A simple beat plays while you improvise freely. |

## 🌊 The Flow: A Typical Practice Session

Here's what a 15-minute practice session might look like:

```
1. Open the app                          (30 seconds)
   └── The ambient sound starts. You settle in.

2. Go to The Player → Fret 1             (2 minutes)
   └── Do the Breathing Gate exercise.
       Breathe with the circle until you feel calm.

3. Go to The Guitar → Pick C             (8 minutes)
   └── Play 2-3 rounds of FLASH mode.
       Memorize patterns, tap from memory.
       Read the tutorial if it's your first time.

4. After 8 rounds → REFLECT appears      (3 minutes)
   └── Read your score breakdown.
       Read Bertrand's coaching message.
       Write a quick journal note.

5. Tap "Done" → Back to Landing          (30 seconds)
   └── Close the app. Come back tomorrow.
```

**There is no penalty for short sessions.** 10 minutes is plenty. The app remembers where you left off.

Voix Vive uses some special terms. Here's what they mean in plain English:

| Term | Plain English | What It Is |

## 🔇 What the App Does NOT Do

This is important. Voix Vive is different from other music apps:

- ❌ **No speed scoring.** You are never punished for being slow.
- ❌ **No leaderboards.** You never compete against other people.
- ❌ **No daily streaks that punish you for missing a day.**
- ❌ **No ads.** Ever.
- ❌ **No required login.** Everything works offline on your device.
- ❌ **No "wrong answer" punishment.** Mistakes are information, not failures.
- ❌ **No performance pressure.** The only person who sees your scores is you.

What it DOES do:
- ✅ Remembers your progress between sessions
- ✅ Gets gradually more complex as you improve
- ✅ Gives you Bertrand's coaching wisdom based on your performance
- ✅ Saves your journal entries privately on your device
- ✅ Works on your phone, tablet, or computer

## ♿ Accessibility Notes

- **Minimum text size:** All text is at least 12.8px (0.8rem). Most body text is 16px.
- **Touch targets:** All interactive elements are at least 48px tall.
- **Color is not the only indicator:** Score feedback uses both color AND text labels.
- **No flashing lights:** Pattern reveals are smooth fades, not strobe effects.
- **Works without a microphone:** All microphone features are optional. You can play the full game in Flash and Imagine modes without a mic.

## 📐 Design Principles Summary

These are the rules the app follows. They explain **why** things look and feel the way they do:

1. **The student is safe.** Nothing in this app judges, rushes, or punishes.
2. **Slow is the point.** Transitions are deliberately unhurried. The ambient music is continuous. This is a practice room, not a competition.
3. **Accuracy over speed.** The game measures how well you see the pattern, not how fast you tap.
4. **Breathing matters.** The app tracks your breath (via microphone) because calm breathing = better learning. This is neuroscience, not mysticism.
5. **The imagination comes first.** Before your fingers move, your mind should see. Before your voice sings, your inner ear should hear.
6. **No comparison.** Your practice is yours. There are no other players to compare against.
7. **10 minutes is enough.** The app is designed for short, focused sessions — not marathon grinding.
