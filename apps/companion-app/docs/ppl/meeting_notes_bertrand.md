# Meeting Notes: Bertrand Laurence — May 27, 2026

## Core Educational Philosophy
- **Somatic & Mindset Integration**: Curriculum bridges somatic trauma treatment, body-centered awareness, neurology, and psychology with guitar learning.
- **Ego as Egg & Shell**: Temporary structure for systematic growth until the learner outgrows it.
- **Allowing Impressions**: Students pause between notes/intervals to observe emotional architecture, imagery, atmosphere. Sound must make a physical, emotional impression.
- **Music by Numbers**: Taught through numbers and ratios — directly correlates to real music theory.

## The Tree Framework (Music Theory)
- **Five Frets First**: Journey starts by mapping the first five frets across all six strings for geographical awareness.
- **Supporting Beams (Triads)**: 1, 3, and 5 scale degrees are the fundamental "supporting beams of Western Harmony."
- **The G-B Glitch**: Standard tuning = perfect fourth between adjacent strings EXCEPT G→B (major third). Learners must consciously compensate.
- **C Scale Foundation**: White keys only — no sharps/flats. Half-steps strictly between 3→4 and 7→8.
- **CAGED System**: Democratic "Rosetta Stone" — 5 interlocking chord shapes (C, A, G, E, D) moving up the fretboard.
- **Flat Five (Blue Note)**: Darkest note, historically banned in Europe. Bridges Western Harmony into blues and metal.
