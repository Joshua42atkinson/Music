## THE CORE AXIOM

> *"You are an instrument playing an instrument. If I am playing the guitar, who is playing me?"*
> — Bertrand Laurence

This axiom governs every design decision. The student is not learning to operate a tool. They are awakening as a somatic musical being.

---

## YIN / YANG DUAL-CODING

Every chapter, every tool, every game phase operates on both axes simultaneously:

| Yin (Invisible) | Yang (Visible) |
|---|---|
| Theory | Fretboard organization |
| Ear training | Muscle memory |
| Meditation | Technique |
| Emotional storytelling | Exercises |
| Inner imagination | Outer performance |

**Design rule:** Every screen must serve both axes. A tool that is purely visual (Yang) without an inner listening element (Yin) is incomplete.

## THE THREE PROPRIETARY PROTOCOLS

These are Bertrand's IP. Use the ©symbol always. Never paraphrase or abbreviate without them.

### ©SHEARL — See / Hear / Feel
- Bridge between theory and technique
- Student perceives the pattern *before* placing fingers
- Anti-pattern: looking at fingers first, playing before imagining
- Platform use: Frets 1–6, Phase 1 of Vertiscale (Inner Fretboard)

### ©PLING! — Sing & Play
- Hardwires vocal cords to motor cortex
- Student sings the pitch *before* playing it
- The exclamation mark is intentional — it is a moment of delight, not command
- Platform use: Frets 3, 7, Phase 2 of Vertiscale (Inner Ear)

### ©FHEAL — Hear / Feel → Obey
- Express freely without Left-Brain interference
- No critic, no correction, no score
- The inner voice that already knows
- Platform use: Frets 8, 10, 11, 12, Phase 3 of Vertiscale (Inner Voice)

**Protocol sequence:** ©SHEARL → ©PLING! → ©FHEAL is the complete learning cycle. It mirrors the Hero's Journey arc of the 12 chapters.

## KEY TEACHING CONCEPTS

- **Practice TOO SLOW** — myelination requires slow repetition, not speed drilling
- **Kinesthetic Sleep** — the plateau before a leap; tell students this is normal
- **The Practice Nook** — dedicated physical space for practice; ritual matters
- **Binder Control** — student manages their own learning arc
- **CAGED System** — chord geometry applied to the neck
- **Vertiscales** — Bertrand's original scale visualization method (vertical = somatic)
- **Notes → Chords → Songs** — the invariant learning sequence
- **Applied Theory** — theory *through* music, never theory *before* music
- **Body-centered practice** — relaxation, effortlessness, flowing ergonomics
- **Student-choice** — students choose songs and styles they love

## SOMATIC GATES (BE → DO → PLAY)

Progression through the curriculum is physically gated. A student cannot advance to the next cognitive concept until the body has proven it understands the current one.
1. **BE (Somatic State / Imagination):** The student must read the theory, imagine the sound, and visualize the pattern without touching the guitar.
2. **DO (Kinesthetic Execution / Pitch):** The student must physically execute the technique or perfectly audiate the pitch (verified via the microphone in the PitchRoom).
3. **PLAY (Flow State / Creativity):** The student must freely express the concept without judgment.

## VERTISCALE GAME — THREE PHASES

The game at Fret 9 is the convergence of all three protocols.

| Phase | Name | Protocol | What Happens | Score Shown? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | The Inner Fretboard | ©SHEARL | Flash patterns → imagine → tap | Yes (internal) |
| Phase 2 | The Inner Ear | ©PLING! | Sing the note → pitch detection | Yes (internal) |
| Phase 3 | The Inner Voice | ©FHEAL | Reflect, journal, express | **No score ever** |

**Difficulty labels** (never Easy/Medium/Hard):
- `Kinesthetic Awakening` (EN) / `Éveil Kinesthésique` (FR)
- `Applied Practice` (EN) / `Pratique Appliquée` (FR)
- `Flow State` (EN) / `État de Flow` (FR)

## SLOW WEB MANDATE

Voix Vive deliberately rejects:
- Streaks that create anxiety
- Leaderboards of any kind
- Speed challenges
- Push notifications
- Dopamine loops

It embraces:
- Deliberate, contemplative practice
- Long-form engagement
- Somatic awareness
- Student-paced progression
- Beauty and depth over novelty
