## What Is This?

We have built a free online academy that teaches your guitar method to anyone in the world.

It is a website (and soon a phone app) called **Voix Vive Academy** — "The Living Voice." Your name, your teaching philosophy, and your curriculum are at the center of everything.

The student opens the app, and your world greets them: your words, your intervals, your exercises, your way of thinking about the guitar as a living instrument.

---

## What Does It Already Do?

Here is what is built and working today:

### 1. The Living Textbook (12 Chapters)
Your entire curriculum is organized into 12 chapters — one per fret of the guitar, one per semitone of the chromatic scale. Each chapter includes:
- **The philosophy** (why this interval matters emotionally)
- **The Pythagorean math** (the ratio, the cents, the ancient origin)
- **The guitar exercise** (what to practice on the fretboard)
- **The body check** (breathing, posture, tension release)

A student can read this for free, in English or French, with no internet required.

### 2. Practice Tools (Built Into the App)
The student does not need to buy any other software. These tools are included:
- **Breathing Gate** — A guided breathing exercise before every practice session
- **Pitch Room** — Uses the phone's microphone to listen to the student sing or play, and tells them if they are on pitch
- **Metronome** — A simple, clean metronome
- **Vertiscale Engine** — Your vertical scale method, turned into an interactive game where the student plays along the string and the app listens
- **Practice Journal** — The student writes reflections after each session

### 3. The Maturation Map
A visual map showing the student's progress across all 12 frets. They can see at a glance: "I have completed Fret 1 through 4. I am working on Fret 5."

### 4. The Troubadour (AI Assistant)
An AI character modeled on Bernard de Ventadorn (the medieval troubadour) that can answer questions about music theory, encourage the student, and guide them through exercises. This works with or without internet — when offline, it uses pre-written coaching prompts instead of live AI.

### 5. Bilingual (English & French)
Every screen, every label, every piece of content works in both English and French.

## What Is the New Idea? (The Game) — Coming Soon

> **Status: Design complete, development pending.** We are designing a practice mode called **The Resonant Mirror**. Here is how it will work in plain language:

1. The student puts the phone down and closes their eyes.
2. They sing one note — any note that feels natural.
3. The phone records that note and plays it back on a loop, like a drone.
4. A voice prompt says: *"Find the Perfect Fifth. The 3:2 ratio."*
5. The student picks up the guitar and plucks the string they think is correct.
6. If they are right, the phone takes their recorded voice and creates a second copy pitched up to the Perfect Fifth. Now they hear a two-part choir of their own voice.
7. The game continues: *"Now find the Major Third."* Another correct pluck, another voice layer. A three-part choir assembles.

**The result:** The student builds a choir of their own voice, one interval at a time, using the guitar as the only control. The screen is off. The ears and the hands do everything.

This teaches your exact curriculum (intervals, ratios, fretboard geometry) but disguises it as a beautiful, meditative puzzle. And because the screen is off, your older students — even those in their 70s and 80s — are not squinting at a tiny phone. They are doing what they came to do: listening, feeling, and playing.

### The Biofeedback Layer (Optional) — Coming Soon
> **Status: Proposed, not yet implemented.** If the student wears a Fitbit or smartwatch, the app can read their heart rate. Before the practice session begins, the app checks: "Are you calm?" If the heart rate is too fast (stress, anxiety), the app guides them through breathing first. It will not let them play until they are relaxed.

This is your Fret 1 teaching in action: *"Trauma creates and keeps tension. You are fighting the instrument."* The app enforces what you teach.

## How Does This Make Money?

The curriculum is **free**. All 12 chapters, all the tools, the game — free.

The money comes from **you**. When a student reaches certain milestones (the end of Act 1, Act 2, or Act 3), the app tells them:

> *"You have learned the foundations. But to cross the threshold, your song must be heard by a Master."*

They then pay for your services:

| Service | Price | What You Do |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Quick Question | $5 | Answer a text question |
| Video Review | $35 | Watch their practice video, send feedback |
| Private Lesson | $65 | 60-minute Zoom call |
| Inner Circle | $25/month | Community access + 1 video review per month |
| Capstone Audition | $100 | Formal review and certificate of completion |

The app is the fishing net. You are the fisherman. The app catches thousands of students for free. The serious ones pay to learn from you directly.
