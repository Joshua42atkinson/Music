## 3. The "Great Game" Connection

Your friend/author wrote *The Great Game: A Player's Handbook to Consciousness*. The Player vs. Character distinction is perfect for your teaching philosophy:

- **The Character** = the guitarist who thinks "I'm bad at barre chords"
- **The Player** = the one who observes that thought without becoming it

This is exactly what you teach when a student gets frustrated. We want to weave this language into the workbench's somatic check-ins.
