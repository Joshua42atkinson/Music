---

## 1. Course Description & Philosophy

*“You are an instrument playing an instrument.”*

Voix Vive Academy is a whole-person music mystery school built upon Pythagorean pedagogy. This course rejects the dopamine-driven, rapid-consumption model of modern e-learning. Instead, it offers a "Slow Web" immersive journey mapping the 12 intervals of the chromatic scale to 12 somatic, emotional, and technical states of being. 

Students will not just learn to play the guitar; they will learn to **Be** (breath and awareness), **Do** (technique and practice), and **Play** (creative flow and performance). The curriculum is guided by a local AI mentor (The Troubadour) and anchored by a human master (Bertrand Laurence).

## 2. Technical Requirements

To fully participate in the Voix Vive Academy, students must have:
- A desktop, laptop, or modern mobile device (Google Chrome recommended).
- A working microphone (required for the **PitchRoom** audiation tools).
- A webcam or smartphone camera (required for **Video Submissions** and Capstone Auditions).
- An acoustic or electric guitar.

## 4. Assessment & The Capstone Rubric

This academy does not use standard letter grades. Progression is tracked via a strict **144-node Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG)**. Students must complete the physical and pedagogical prerequisites (the BE/DO/PLAY Somatic Gates) before advancing to the next conceptual phase.

For intentional practice and non-linear exploration, students may activate **Sandbox Mode** via the Troubadour Widget, which temporarily lifts these restrictions without corrupting their saved progression.

To advance through the formal hierarchy (Apprentice → Journeyman → Master), students must submit a **Capstone Audition Video** to Bertrand Laurence.

### Capstone Grading Rubric

Submissions are evaluated on a 3-point mastery scale across the three pedagogical pillars:

| Pillar | Emerging (Still Searching) | Competent (Finding the Path) | Masterful (The Troubadour) |
|--------|----------------------------|------------------------------|----------------------------|
| **BE (Somatic State)** | Student exhibits visible physical tension. Breath is shallow or disconnected from the music. | Posture is generally relaxed. Breath aligns with musical phrasing, though focus occasionally breaks. | Absolute physical ease. The student and the instrument move as one continuous system. |
| **DO (Technical Execution)** | Notes are missed or muted. Rhythm is inconsistent or rushed. Ear training pitch is frequently off. | Notes ring clearly. Rhythm is stable. Pitch matching is mostly accurate with minor corrections. | Flawless technical execution without conscious effort. Perfect intonation and timing. |
| **PLAY (Creative Flow)** | Performance feels mechanical or recited. Emotional intent does not match the interval's character. | Performance expresses genuine emotion. The character of the fret is clearly communicated. | The performance transcends the notes. The student is speaking fluently through the instrument. |

## 5. Mentorship & SLA (Service Level Agreement)

While the 132-node curriculum and AI Troubadour are available 24/7, human mentorship is subject to the following response times:
- **Quick Questions (Text Feedback)**: Guaranteed response from Bertrand within 48 business hours.
- **Video Reviews & Capstones**: Guaranteed personalized video critique within 7 calendar days.
- **Live Zoom Lessons**: Must be scheduled at least 24 hours in advance via the Mentor Portal.

## 6. Academic Policies

- **Asynchronous Integrity**: The AI Troubadour is a guide, not a shortcut. Students are expected to complete the physical and journaling tasks honestly before marking a node as complete.
- **Video Privacy**: All practice videos uploaded via the Coaching Portal are stored securely in the student's personal Google Drive and are only accessible by Bertrand Laurence. They will never be shared publicly without explicit written consent.
- **Accessibility**: The academy relies heavily on visual color-coding and audio pitch detection. Students requiring accommodations (e.g., colorblind UI modes or alternative pitch feedback) should contact the platform architect.
