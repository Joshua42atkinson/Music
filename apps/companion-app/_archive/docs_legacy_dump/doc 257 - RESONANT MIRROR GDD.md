# THE RESONANT MIRROR: Voix Vive Game Engine
*A Sovereign, Screenless, Audio-First Pedagogical Game*

---

## 1. The Core Paradigm
The greatest friction in digital music education is the screen. It forces the eyes open, locks the posture, and pulls attention away from the instrument and the body. 

**The Resonant Mirror** flips this paradigm entirely:
*   **The Screen:** Off. (Or displaying a minimalist, ambient holding state).
*   **The Game Board:** The acoustic space of the room.
*   **The Controller:** The Guitar fretboard.
*   **The Avatar:** The player's own recorded voice.

This is not a "gamified web app." It is an auditory augmented-reality puzzle that uses the exact Pythagorean math of the Spanish Classical system to drive progression.

---

## 2. The Core Game Loop (Auditory Alchemy)

The game is played in a recursive loop of Call, Response, and Harmony.

### Phase I: The Seed (Singing)
The player puts the phone on the music stand and closes their eyes. 
The system (guided by the Troubadour's voice) asks for a seed: *"Give me your root note."*
The player sings a sustained tone (*Ahhhh*). The microphone captures a 2-second buffer of their exact biological frequency. The system then begins looping this note continuously beneath the game, serving as the Drone (The 1:1 Ratio).

### Phase II: The Quest (Listening)
The system issues an auditory challenge based on the curriculum. 
*"The Root is lonely. Find the Perfect Fifth—the 3:2 ratio. The great architectural beam. Show it to me."*
The player must mentally calculate where the Perfect Fifth of their root note lives on the fretboard geometry. 

### Phase III: The Input (Playing)
The player does not touch a screen. They use the guitar as the gamepad. They pluck the string they believe holds the Perfect Fifth. 
The game's pitch detector analyzes the frequency of the wood and string in real-time.

### Phase IV: The Reward (The Magic)
If the player plucks the wrong string, the game subtly responds: *"That is the minor third. Tension. We seek the Fifth."*
If the player plucks the correct string, **the magic happens**. 
The engine instantly takes the 2-second recording of the player's own voice, pitch-shifts it up by exactly the 3:2 ratio, and layers it perfectly over the root drone. 
Suddenly, the player is listening to a beautiful, two-part choir of *themselves*, unlocked entirely by their physical mastery of the instrument.

## 3. Pedagogical Camouflage (Hiding the Math)

Bertrand's system relies on deep memorization of intervals, geometry, and Pythagorean ratios (Spanish Classical fundamentals). Left alone, this is rote, pedantic memorization.

In the Resonant Mirror, the math is camouflaged as **Spellcasting**.
*   You are not "memorizing intervals." You are trying to build a 6-part choir of your own voice.
*   To unlock the "Phrygian dominant b2" harmony, your fingers must know exactly where it lives on the neck in a split second.
*   The adrenaline of "answering the riddle" with your hands burns the fretboard map into your nervous system faster than any visual chart ever could.

## 4. The Meta-Game & Monetization (The Campaign)

### The 12-Fret Overworld
While the gameplay is screenless, the session begins and ends on a visual **Overworld Map** (The 12-Fret Hero's Journey). 
*   Each node on the map represents an auditory encounter. 
*   Act I (Frets 1-4) teaches the foundational choir (Unison to Perfect Fourth).
*   Act II (Frets 5-8) introduces the demons (The Tritone, the minor second) where the player must intentionally create and resolve dissonance with their voice and guitar.

### The Mentor Gate
To graduate from an Act, the player must face a "Boss Battle" (The Song Milestone). 
The game challenges them to build a full progression without prompts, using everything they've learned, and record it. 
**This is the monetization funnel:** To pass the milestone and unlock the next map region, the recording must be reviewed by the Mentor (Bertrand / Joshua). The player pays for the sovereign mentorship key, bridging the gap between automated gameplay and elite human transmission.

## 5. The Somatic Feedback Loop (Fitbit Integration)

Since the Voix Vive architecture already utilizes the Google Ecosystem (Login, Drive, Calendar), we can integrate the **Fitbit Web API** to turn the player's physical nervous system into a game mechanic. 

Bertrand's core teaching (Fret 1: The Root) states: *"Trauma creates and keeps tension. You are fighting the instrument."* To play the Spanish Classical guitar properly, the student must be in the "Ventral Vagal State" (rest and digest).

**How Fitbit Gamifies the Body:**
1.  **The Breathing Gate:** Before the session begins, the game reads the player's real-time Heart Rate (HR) and Heart Rate Variability (HRV) via Fitbit. If the heart rate is elevated (stress), the game initiates a somatic breathing exercise. The game *will not* unlock the fretboard challenges until the Fitbit confirms the heart rate has slowed to a state of calm.
2.  **Dynamic Tension Detection:** As the player attempts complex ratios (like the Tritone), if they begin to hold their breath or tense up, their heart rate spikes. The game detects this biometric shift and gently pauses the loop: *"Your body is fighting the dissonance. Release the shoulder. Breathe. Let the tension resolve."*
3.  **The Calibration:** The player quickly learns that they cannot brute-force the game. The only way to win is to biologically relax.

This makes the Voix Vive Academy the first true **Bio-Feedback EdTech Game**, where the player's autonomic nervous system is an active input in the gameplay.

## 6. Why This Works

1.  **Sovereign & Offline:** It requires zero cloud AI to run. Web Audio API and local pitch detection happen entirely on-device, in the woods, with no internet.
2.  **Somatic Sovereignty:** By turning off the screen, the player is forced back into their body. They must rely on their ears and their kinesthetic memory.
3.  **The "Wow" Factor:** Hearing a choir of your own voice organically assemble itself in real-time, controlled only by the wood and wire in your hands, is a deeply emotional, jaw-dropping UX that no other app provides.
