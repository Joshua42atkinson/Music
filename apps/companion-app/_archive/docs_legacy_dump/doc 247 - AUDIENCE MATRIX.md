# Audience Matrix: Age & Cognitive Alignment

After a full review of the curriculum (`chapterData.js`), the philosophical depth, linguistic complexity, and integration of music, physics, and psychology strictly define the target audience for Voix Vive.

## Target Audience: Ages 14+ (High School & Adult Learners)

The curriculum is **not** suitable for elementary or early middle school students (ages 6-12). It is highly abstract, existential, and demands self-reflection. 

However, it is an **absolute masterpiece for the High School Homeschool market**. 

### Why Ages 14+ is the Perfect Fit

#### 1. The Existential Themes
* **Content:** Concepts like "The Inner Critic," "Ego," "Nigredo (Alchemical Blackening)," and "Trauma holding tension."
* **Cognitive Alignment:** Around age 14, teenagers enter a stage of deep existential questioning and self-awareness. They are dealing with anxiety, identity, and their "inner critic." The curriculum directly addresses this psychological reality through the guitar, turning music practice into a somatic therapy tool.

#### 2. The Interdisciplinary Academics
* **Content:** Pythagorean tuning ratios (256:243), the Ventral Vagal nervous system, geometry, and frequency cents.
* **Cognitive Alignment:** This is high-school-level Physics, Anatomy, and Mathematics disguised as guitar lessons. For a homeschooling parent, this curriculum checks multiple boxes: Music, Philosophy, and Applied Science.

#### 3. The Vocabulary
* **Content:** Terms like *Kinesthesis, Fascia, Dissonance, Wu Wei, Audiation.*
* **Cognitive Alignment:** Requires a 9th-grade reading level or higher. The Troubadour AI (The Voix layer) is perfectly suited to explain these terms Socratically to a teenager without talking down to them.

---

## What Needs to Change? (Nothing)
You do not need to rewrite the curriculum to "dumb it down" for kids. You simply need to position it correctly. 

**Marketing Positioning:**
* *Not:* "Learn guitar online."
* *Instead:* "An interdisciplinary high-school curriculum combining Music Theory, Acoustic Physics, and Somatic Psychology."

If a parent has a 15-year-old who is dealing with anxiety or struggling to find focus, a daily curriculum that combines Morning Meditation (Souffle), Cognitive Theory (Voix), and Physical Musical Expression (Chant) is exactly what they are looking for.
