Manège à Trois :- not Ménage ;) the trio has been together informally for well over ten years primarily to play Musette, a 1900’s Parisian style of street dance music.

The song list quickly expanded to other genres, specially the “Chanson” influenced by American iconic styles of Blues, Folk and Jazz, and covering 8 decades of French Pop.

So come celebrate  both the French Language and music and it’s American influence,

while expat Singer/Guitarist Bertrand Laurence, cures his "Mal du Pays ' (homesick Blues) with a unique collection of French Songs from the 20’s on.

Expect a flavorful Gumbo of American Jazz and Folk in French, French Blues in English, and everything in between.

With the splendid Suzy Lee on Accordéon, and Mark Chênevert on Crinet. ( Oui, il a perdu le LA )
