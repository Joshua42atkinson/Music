First, feed your nails: 
My fav nail  food is

not a toxic varnish, but actual food.

Pure Vitamin E oil is also great and will deal with dry, brittle nails, a common problem in winter

Shape and smooth nails  from cuticle corner towards the center of nail.

Polish, then Buff the edge to a glassy smoothness with a  super fine grit nail file:

Better string-connection,  tone, attack less breakage due to nail fiber getting caught.
Look for an even, consistent sound , feel and volume throughout your picking fingers

Micro Finishing paper will do a great job finishing your nail to a glassy smooth edge.
(2000 grit is so fine you cant even see/feel the grain)

You could glue them on those CVS sanding blocks once they are dull.
